# Seoul campus evidence — v0.5

Reviewed October 1, 2026 (Korea). This is an independent student guide.

The [official Humanities campus directory](https://www.mju.ac.kr/campusMap/mjukr/view.do?findType=A) supplies 11 named entries: eight buildings (S1, S2, S3, S4, S5, S8, S9 and S10), sports field, gate and gatehouse. The directory JSON was obtained from its `/campusMap/mjukr/1/artclList.do` endpoint. Source markers and review notes are preserved in [the evidence record](../data/seoul-reference-evidence.json).

Directory markers are projected using WGS84 local east/north coordinates at 0.46 scene units per metre. Their relative marker distances are preserved. They are points of interest, not surveyed wall centres. Footprints, heights, trees, paving and facade repetition are expressly schematic. No surveyed Seoul site plan was found; Yongin’s historical plan is not reused for Seoul. No walking route, entrance geometry, indoor route or walking time is invented.

The scene interprets the [university’s illustrated overview, page 6](https://www.mju.ac.kr/bbs/record/359/168757/download.do) and [official campus photograph](https://gsca.mju.ac.kr/CrossEditor/binary/images/000355/20251128163849970_107N73HV.png). The university images remain external reference links and are not redistributed as app textures. The campus is not presented as a complete measured reconstruction of every current structure.

The [2024 building-number guide](https://www.mju.ac.kr/bbs/ice1/1157/156902/download.do) supports the listed building numbers. A [dated 2026 explanation](https://www.mju.ac.kr/bbs/mjukr/466/234268/artclView.do) uses S10303 for MCC/S10. The undated classroom guide’s conflicting S11 code is disclosed and not automatically resolved. Building-code lookup does not prove room existence or a student's class assignment.

Four food facilities come from the [official Seoul welfare list](https://www.mju.ac.kr/mjukr/489/subview.do). Generic facilities retain descriptive names instead of invented brands. Normal opening hours, current prices and dietary suitability remain unknown. A separate dated breakfast notice is cited only for its published programme conditions.

The [September 30 notice](https://www.mju.ac.kr/bbs/mjukr/141/235890/artclView.do) ends the trial Seoul–Yongin and Sadang–Yongin shuttles from October 1, 2026. Seoul has no active departure timetable saved. It shows this notice and the [official public transport directions](https://www.mju.ac.kr/mjukr/508/subview.do). These are route references, not live arrivals. Yongin’s separate regular semester timetables remain unchanged.

English and Simplified Chinese names are descriptive translations. Original Korean names and source links remain available. The supplied Yongin Notion export is not treated as evidence for Seoul. Campus-specific lists, chat histories, search, food, bus information and remote content updates stay isolated.
