# Seoul vegetation — 9 November 2026

Seoul planting follows the areas visible in the university's campus illustration and corroborating university photographs. The previous generic board-edge rows are replaced by restrained groups around the raised sports terrace/MCC and the entrance approach. The presentation-board edge is not a campus boundary.

## Sources and certainty

- [University campus tour](https://www.mju.ac.kr/mjukr/12348/subview.do) and its [1200 × 818 illustration](https://www.mju.ac.kr/sites/mjukr/images/temp_1783296837696100.png): terrace margins, MCC street gardens, entrance island and small gate-side planting pockets.
- [University newspaper, 10 October 2023](https://news.mju.ac.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=12317): real photographs of MCC's planted frontage and leafy trees in the narrow approach from the gate toward Student Union.
- [University notice with the MCC panorama](https://welfare.mju.ac.kr/bbs/welfare/243/227771/artclView.do): [photograph](https://gsca.mju.ac.kr/CrossEditor/binary/images/000355/20251128163849970_107N73HV.png) corroborates young trees on the elevated terrace and planted gardens below. Publication on 28 November 2025 does not establish the photograph's capture date.

All fixed source URLs were accessible on 9 November 2026. The reviewed image hashes match the existing reference archive. Source access is not an on-site survey or confirmation that each tree remains present today. The KLEC overview repeats the same illustration and is not an independent survey.

[Machine-readable evidence](../data/seoul-vegetation-evidence.json) records source URLs, image dimensions, hashes and seven area-level image regions. These rectangles refer to the oblique illustration; they must not be interpreted as georeferenced polygons or transformed into surveyed tree coordinates.

The renderer uses generic leafy canopies and low planting. Its positions, counts, crown sizes, heights and bed outlines are symbolic display choices within those areas. Species, individual trunk positions, mature heights and present-day counts are unknown. Narrow existing pitch margins limit the symbolic terrace crowns; the pitch and surrounding buildings are not resized to accommodate trees.
